# Glossary of Terms

**Advanced Chama Management System** — Plain English definitions.

---

**Account** — A category in the chart of accounts used for double-entry bookkeeping (e.g., "Cash at Bank", "Loan Receivables").

**Activity Log** — A record of user actions (logins, page visits) used for monitoring.

**Agenda** — A list of topics or items to be discussed during a meeting.

**Amortization** — The process of paying off a loan through regular installments over time.

**Announcement** — A broadcast message visible to all users or a specific group role.

**Attendance** — A record of which members were present at a meeting.

**Audit Log** — A detailed record of every change made to data, including who made it, what was changed, and when.

**Authentication** — The process of verifying a user's identity (logging in).

**Authorization** — The process of determining what a user is allowed to do (permissions).

**Balance** — The remaining amount owed on a loan, or the current value of an account.

**Beneficiary** — A person designated to receive benefits, such as upon a member's death (not fully implemented).

**Breadcrumb** — A navigation trail showing the current page location (not implemented in this system).

**Certificate** — A unique number assigned to each share purchase for identification.

**Chama** — An informal savings and investment group, common in Kenya and East Africa.

**Chart of Accounts** — The complete list of financial accounts used in the group's bookkeeping system.

**Collateral** — An asset (e.g., land title, vehicle logbook) pledged as security for a loan.

**Contribution** — Money paid by a member into the group's savings pool.

**Contribution Type** — A category of contribution with its own amount and frequency (e.g., Monthly Savings, Welfare Fund).

**Credit** — In double-entry accounting, an entry on the right side that decreases assets or increases liabilities.

**CSRF (Cross-Site Request Forgery)** — A security attack where a malicious website tricks a user into submitting a request. The system uses CSRF tokens to prevent this.

**CSRF Token** — A unique, hidden value in each form that verifies the form submission came from the legitimate site.

**Dashboard** — The main overview page displayed after login, showing key statistics and recent activity.

**Debit** — In double-entry accounting, an entry on the left side that increases assets or decreases liabilities.

**Disbursement** — The release of approved loan funds to a borrower.

**Dividend** — A share of profits distributed to members who own shares (not fully implemented).

**Double-Entry** — An accounting method where every transaction affects at least two accounts, with total debits equalling total credits.

**Expense** — Money spent by the group on operations (e.g., transport, stationery, rent).

**Favicon** — A small icon displayed in the browser tab.

**Flash Message** — A temporary notification that appears at the top of a page after an action (e.g., "Member saved successfully").

**Group Code** — A unique identifier shared by all users and members of the same chama group.

**Guarantor** — A member who agrees to repay a loan if the primary borrower defaults.

**Income** — Money received by the group (e.g., loan interest, registration fees).

**Journal Entry** — A double-entry accounting record that debits one or more accounts and credits others.

**Ledger** — A page or view showing all transactions for a specific account.

**Loan Product** — A predefined loan type with specific terms: interest rate, amount limits, tenure, fees.

**Member** — An individual who belongs to the chama group.

**Member Number** — A unique identifier automatically assigned to each member (e.g., CHM-2024-0001).

**Minutes** — The official written record of what was discussed, decided, and actioned in a meeting.

**Notification** — An in-app alert informing a user about an event (e.g., loan approved, new announcement).

**Pagination** — Splitting a long list of records across multiple numbered pages.

**PDO (PHP Data Objects)** — A database access layer used by the system for secure SQL queries.

**Permission** — A specific action a user is allowed to perform (e.g., `create_members`, `approve_loans`).

**Prepared Statement** — A secure way to execute SQL queries by separating the query structure from the data.

**Receipt Number** — A unique identifier automatically generated for each contribution or payment transaction.

**Role** — A job function (e.g., Treasurer, Secretary) that determines which permissions a user has.

**Role Slug** — A short, machine-readable version of the role name (e.g., `loan_officer`, `super_admin`).

**Schedule** — A loan repayment plan showing each installment's due date, amounts, and remaining balance.

**Session** — A period of active login, during which the user's state is maintained on the server.

**Share** — A unit of ownership in the group's investment pool, purchased by members.

**SMTP (Simple Mail Transfer Protocol)** — The standard protocol for sending emails. Settings exist but are not functional.

**Soft Delete** — Marking a record as inactive instead of permanently deleting it from the database.

**Tenure** — The duration of a loan, typically expressed in months.

**Trial Balance** — An accounting report listing all accounts with their total debits and credits to verify they balance.

**Upsert** — A database operation that inserts a new record or updates an existing one if it already exists (INSERT ... ON DUPLICATE KEY UPDATE).
