# Member Guide

**Advanced Chama Management System** — For regular members.

---

## 1. Your Account

As a member, you have a limited view of the system. You can:

- View your **Dashboard** with your personal stats
- See your **Contributions** history
- View your **Loans** and loan details
- See your **Shares** and certificates
- Read **Announcements**
- Send and receive **Messages**
- View **Meetings** and your attendance
- Update your **Profile** and password

You **cannot** add members, record contributions, approve loans, manage settings, or view audit logs.

---

## 2. Logging In

1. Go to the system URL in your browser.
2. Click **"Sign In"**.
3. Enter:
   - **Email** — The email your administrator registered for you
   - **Password** — Your password (default: `Admin@123`, change on first login)
   - **Group Code** — Provided by your administrator
4. Click **"Sign In"**.

---

## 3. Your Dashboard

After logging in, you will see:

- **Your stats** — Your total contributions, active loans, and shares
- **Recent activities** — Latest contributions and loans
- **Upcoming meetings** — Meetings you should attend
- **Notifications** — Recent alerts about your account

---

## 4. Viewing Your Contributions

1. Click **Contributions** in the sidebar.
2. You will see a table of all your recorded contributions.
3. Each entry shows:
   - Contribution type (e.g., Monthly Savings)
   - Amount paid
   - Date
   - Payment method
   - Receipt number
   - Payment reference

---

## 5. Viewing Your Shares

1. Click **Shares** in the sidebar.
2. You will see all your share purchases.
3. Each entry shows:
   - Share product (e.g., Ordinary Shares)
   - Number of shares
   - Total amount paid
   - Certificate number
   - Purchase date

---

## 6. Viewing Your Loans

1. Click **Loans** in the sidebar, then **Active Loans**.
2. You will see all loans associated with you.
3. Click a loan to view:
   - **Loan details** — Amount, interest rate, tenure, purpose
   - **Repayment schedule** — Upcoming installments
   - **Payment history** — All payments made
   - **Guarantors** — Who is guaranteeing your loan
   - **Collateral** — Any items pledged

---

## 7. Messages

1. Click **Messaging** in the sidebar.
2. **Inbox** tab shows messages sent to you.
3. **Sent** tab shows messages you have sent.
4. Click **"Compose"** to send a new message.

---

## 8. Announcements

1. Click **Announcements** in the sidebar.
2. All published announcements are shown as cards.
3. Pinned announcements appear first.
4. Priority badges (Normal, High, Urgent) indicate importance.

---

## 9. Meetings

1. Click **Meetings** in the sidebar.
2. View scheduled meetings, your attendance records, and meeting minutes.
3. Your attendance status (present, absent, excused, late) is recorded for each meeting.

---

## 10. Updating Your Profile

1. Click your name in the top-right corner, then **"My Profile"**.
2. You can update:
   - **Username** — Your display name
   - **Email** — Your email address
   - **Phone** — Your phone number
   - **Photo** — Upload a profile picture

---

## 11. Changing Your Password

1. Go to **Profile**.
2. Scroll to **"Change Password"**.
3. Enter your current password and a new password.
4. Your new password must:
   - Be at least 8 characters long
   - Contain uppercase and lowercase letters
   - Contain at least one number
   - Contain at least one special character
5. Click **"Update Password"**.

---

## 12. Logging Out

Click your name in the top-right corner, then **"Sign Out"**.

---

## Tips for Members

- Check **Announcements** regularly for important group updates.
- View your **Dashboard** to stay updated on your financial position.
- Use **Messaging** to contact group officials directly.
- If you cannot log in, contact your group administrator.
- Report any errors or unusual data to the secretary or treasurer.
