# Quick Start Guide

**Advanced Chama Management System** — Get up and running in 5 minutes.

---

## 1. Log In

1. Open your browser and go to the system URL.
2. Click **"Sign In"**.
3. Enter your credentials:

   | Field | Demo Value |
   |-------|-----------|
   | Email | `admin@chama.com` |
   | Password | `Admin@123` |
   | Group Code | `CHAMA001` |

4. Click **"Sign In"**.

## 2. Dashboard

After login, you will see the **Dashboard** with:

- **4 stat cards** (Members, Contributions, Loans, Shares)
- **Charts** (Contribution trends, Loan status)
- **Recent activity** tables
- **Sidebar** with all menu items on the left

---

## 3. Add a Member

1. Click **Members** in the sidebar.
2. Click **"Add New Member"**.
3. Enter:
   - **First Name** and **Last Name** (required)
   - **Phone** (required)
   - **Date Joined** (required)
4. Click **"Save Member"**.

---

## 4. Record a Contribution

1. Click **Contributions** in the sidebar.
2. Click **"Record Contribution"**.
3. Select the **Member** and **Contribution Type**.
4. Enter the **Amount**.
5. Click **"Save"**.

---

## 5. Apply for a Loan

1. Click **Loans > Applications** in the sidebar.
2. Click **"New Application"**.
3. Select the **Member** and **Loan Product**.
4. Enter the **Amount**, **Tenure**, and **Purpose**.
5. Click **"Submit Application"**.

---

## 6. Approve and Disburse

1. Open the pending loan from **Loans > Applications**.
2. Approve at your level (Secretary → Treasurer → Chairperson).
3. Once fully approved, the **Loan Officer** clicks **"Disburse"**.

---

## 7. Record Loan Repayment

1. Click **Loans > Repayments** in the sidebar.
2. Click **"Record Payment"**.
3. Select the **Loan**.
4. Enter **Amount**, **Principal**, and **Interest** portions.
5. Click **"Save"**.

---

## 8. Schedule a Meeting

1. Click **Meetings > Schedule Meeting** in the sidebar.
2. Enter the **Title**, **Date**, **Time**, and **Venue**.
3. Add **Agenda items** (optional).
4. Click **"Save Meeting"**.

---

## 9. Export a Report

1. Click **Reports** in the sidebar, then choose a report type.
2. Set filters (date range, etc.).
3. Click **"Export PDF"**.

---

## 10. Log Out

Click your name in the top-right corner → **"Sign Out"**.
